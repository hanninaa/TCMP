# TCMPHal: A Large-scale Dataset for Hallucination Detection in Traditional Chinese Medicine Pharmacy

The proliferation of Large Language Models
(LLMs) in medicine highlights their potential
in Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM). However, the hallucination challenge arises when LLMs generate content that appears plausible yet inaccurate. Motivated by this gap, we introduce TCMPHal, the first dataset designed for hallucination detection in TCM pharmacy, featuring 10,000 high-quality question-answer
pairs with hallucination annotations. Our experimental results on current LLMs across standard, knowledge-based, and search engine settings reveal both the capabilities and limitations of these models on this task. We further conduct an in-depth error analysis to point out potential avenues for future developments.

The *dataset* is the TCMPHal.
